Terms of Service

The fine print.

Last updated: October 2026

This is a concept demonstration site — not a real business. So the short version: there is nothing to buy here, nothing you type is sent anywhere, and nothing on this page is a contract with anyone.

The demo form

The booking request form is a working prototype with no backend. What you type stays in your browser and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. The confirmation message you see is generated locally, on your device.

Illustrative content

All names, flash designs, prices, reviews, hours, and studio details on this site are illustrative. Nothing here is an offer of services, and no booking made on this demo creates an obligation on either side.

What a real studio's terms would cover

Before this concept became a live studio site, a real policy would need to spell out: minimum client age, consultation vs. confirmed appointment, deposit and cancellation terms, design approval before ink, aftercare responsibilities, and who may use photos of finished work. None of those exist here yet — they would be the studio's real policies, written by the studio, not invented for the demo.
